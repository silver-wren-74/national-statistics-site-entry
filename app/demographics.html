Population

Indonesia’s population reached 283,487,931 in 2024 (UN World Population Prospects 2024, medium variant), up 0.82% on 2023. The World Bank records 285.7 million for 2025. About 59% of Indonesians live in towns and cities, and the population density is roughly 149 people per square kilometre of land.

Age structure

Indonesia is still a young country: almost a quarter of the population is under 15, and more than two in three are of working age (15–64). The old-age dependency ratio is only 10.7 — about 11 people aged 65+ for every 100 of working age — while the child dependency ratio is 36.1.

Largest provinces

Java is home to more than half of all Indonesians. West Java alone, with about 49.9 million people, would be one of the world’s 30 most populous countries on its own. Figures are BPS mid-year estimates for 2024.
