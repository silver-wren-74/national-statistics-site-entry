Growth and size

Indonesia’s economy grew 5.03% in real terms in 2024 (BPS), the fourth straight year of growth at or above 5% after the 2020 pandemic contraction of 2.07%. Nominal GDP reached $1.40 trillion (World Bank), the world’s 16th largest economy, or $4.67 trillion on a purchasing-power-parity basis. GDP per person was $4,925 — $16,515 in PPP terms.

What the economy is made of

Manufacturing is the largest single sector at 18.7% of GDP, followed by wholesale and retail trade (12.9%), agriculture, forestry and fishing (12.5%), construction (9.7%) and mining and quarrying (8.6%). The nickel-processing boom has lifted manufacturing’s share since 2020. Shares are BPS estimates for 2024.

Key indicators

Macroeconomic indicators

Reading the numbers. Inflation of 1.57% in 2024 was the lowest on record and inside Bank Indonesia’s 2.5% ± 1% target band, while unemployment fell to 4.91% — the lowest since the pandemic. Poverty, at 8.57% in September 2024, is at its lowest level ever recorded by BPS.
