Indonesia at a glance

Indonesia is an archipelago of more than 17,000 islands and 38 provinces, stretching across three time zones between the Indian and Pacific Oceans. With 283.5 million people in 2024 it is the world’s fourth most populous country and Southeast Asia’s largest economy. Choose a headline figure below to open its full section.

Indonesia in the world

Indonesia is the world’s fourth most populous country, its 14th largest by land area, and its 16th largest economy — 8th by purchasing power parity. Its goods exports rank 28th in the world.
