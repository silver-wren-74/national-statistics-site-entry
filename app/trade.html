National Statistics · Indonesia

Trade

Indonesia exported $264.7 billion of goods in 2024 and imported $233.0 billion — a surplus of about $31.7 billion, the fifth in a row.

What Indonesia sells

Mineral fuels and oils are the biggest export group at $55.5 billion (21.0% of goods exports), led by coal — Indonesia is the world’s largest thermal coal exporter — then animal and vegetable oils, mostly palm oil, at $26.8 billion, and iron and steel at $25.8 billion, a category transformed by nickel processing. Non-oil and gas products made up 86.1% of exports in 2024.

Who buys it

China takes almost a quarter of Indonesia’s goods exports ($62.3 billion, 23.5%), buying coal, palm oil, nickel and steel. The United States is second at $28.4 billion — mainly footwear, electronics, textiles, rubber and shrimp — followed by India at $21.5 billion.
